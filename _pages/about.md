I am a Bioinformatics Programmer II at [UC San Diego – Gaulton Lab](https://gaultonlab.org/) in La Jolla, CA, where I build large-scale computational pipelines for cis-regulatory element mapping and cell-cell communication analysis. My work directly supports the [IGVF Consortium](https://igvf.org/), [PanKBase](https://pankbase.org/), and ongoing multi-tissue genomics initiatives (heart, liver, pancreas, lung).

Previously, I was a Bioinformatics Analyst at [Stoke Therapeutics](https://www.stoketherapeutics.com/), where I owned the lab's core NGS workflows (RNA-seq, splicing, qPCR) and architected cloud-scale sequencing infrastructure on AWS. Before that, I contributed to genomics and drug discovery work at AbbVie (in collaboration with Google Calico Labs) and to immuno-oncology target identification at Merck KGaA (EMD Serono).

I hold an M.S. in Bioinformatics from Northeastern University and a B.Tech in Biotechnology from Vellore Institute of Technology.

## Research Interests

Single-cell genomics, cis-regulatory element biology, cell-cell communication, differential expression, and reproducible pipeline development for large-scale multi-omics data.

## Personal Interests

Reading, language learning, hiking, and exploring the San Diego food scene.
